Privacy Policy

The application communicates with a secure server to store and retrieve project-related information. This may include user inputs, assignments, and updates required for proper functionality of the app.

Device Access

The app may access limited device features such as storage or notifications to improve user experience. No unnecessary or unrelated device data is accessed.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

Children’s Privacy

Our application is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children without parental consent.
