Privacy Policy

This privacy policy governs the use of our application available on the Apple App Store. This application prioritizes user privacy and are designed to offer a simple and secure gaming experience for players of all ages.

Information Collection and Use

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal data or identifiable information. The games are built to function entirely offline, meaning no internet connection is required or used during gameplay. Players can enjoy our game without concerns about data tracking or sharing.

Device Access

This application may access limited device resources solely to enhance the gaming experience (e.g., sound, vibration), but no data or user-specific information is collected or stored. The game does not interact with other applications, contacts, or user data on the device.

Data Security

Since our game does not collect or transmit any user data, there are no security risks related to personal information. The games operate entirely on the user’s device, ensuring privacy and security. There is no remote storage or data transfer of any kind.

Children’s Privacy

Our game is designed to be safe and suitable for users of all ages, including children. Parents and guardians can be assured that there is no exposure to online risks or unwanted data collection, as our game maintains an offline, secure environment without interactions beyond the device.

For any questions regarding this privacy policy, feel free to reach out to the app support team.
