PROTECTION PROTOCOLS

Lock Down Your Digital Life

Core security hygiene. Each one blocks a real attack vector.

🔑
PASSPHRASE PROTOCOL
Use 4+ random words instead of passwords. "correct-horse-battery-staple" has 44 bits of entropy vs 6 for "P@ss1".
📱
2FA EVERYWHERE
Authenticator apps (not SMS) add a layer even stolen passwords can't bypass. Covers 99.9% of automated attacks.
🔒
PASSWORD MANAGER
Unique password per site. One master key. Humans reuse passwords — attackers rely on this. Break the pattern.
🔄
PATCH IMMEDIATELY
60% of breaches exploit vulnerabilities with patches already available. Update within 24h of critical patch release.
📡
VPN ON PUBLIC WiFi
Coffee shop WiFi is a hunting ground. Man-in-the-middle attacks are trivial on open networks without encryption.
🎭
SOCIAL ENG. DEFENSE
Verify unexpected requests via a separate channel. Urgency + authority + unusual request = classic social engineering.
💾
3-2-1 BACKUP RULE
3 copies, 2 different media, 1 offsite. Ransomware's only power is your lack of backup. Remove that power.
🔍
BREACH MONITORING
Monitor your email at HaveIBeenPwned. Time between breach and credential use averages 15 months — act fast.
SCAM PATTERN RECOGNITION

Spot the Attack Vector

Real scam patterns. Identify the threat before you click Reveal.

⚠ ANALYZE
// EMAIL
Your Apple ID has been suspended
Dear Customer, unusual activity was detected. Verify your account within 24 hours or it will be permanently deleted. Click here: appleid-verify-secure.net
🔴 PHISHING — Domain "appleid-verify-secure.net" is NOT apple.com. Urgency tactics + fake domain = credential theft attempt. Never click links in unsolicited emails.
◈ ANALYZE
// SMS
Your order has shipped — track it
Hi! Your Amazon order #112-4829301 has shipped. Track your package: amazon.com/track?id=4829301. Est. delivery: Fri Dec 8.
🟢 LEGITIMATE — Domain is amazon.com (not a lookalike). No urgency pressure, no request for credentials or payment. Order ID matches real Amazon format. Safe to click.
⚠ ANALYZE
// PHONE CALL
"This is Microsoft Support..."
"We detected a virus on your computer. We need remote access immediately to remove it before your data is stolen. Please install this software now."
🔴 VISHING SCAM — Microsoft never calls unsolicited. "Remote access" + "install this software" = ransomware/spyware delivery. Hang up immediately. Legitimate companies don't call to demand access.
⚠ ANALYZE
// SOCIAL MEDIA
Investment returns of 40% monthly — verified
I turned $500 into $12,000 in 3 months using this crypto platform. DM me for the link. Limited spots. My friends are all doing it and cashing out daily 💰
🔴 CRYPTO SCAM — "40% monthly" is mathematically unsustainable. "Limited spots" + "DM me" are classic pressure tactics. These are often pig-butchering scams or Ponzi schemes. No legitimate investment operates this way.
