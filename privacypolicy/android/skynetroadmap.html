Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application Skynet RoadMap. This application is designed to help students and learners track their learning progress, goals, and roadmap activities in a simple and user-friendly way.

Information We Collect

Skynet RoadMap does not share sensitive personal user data. All learning progress and roadmap data remain on the user's device.

  • Roadmap progress
  • Task completion details
  • Learning stages
  • Daily activity tracking
  • Goal progress information

We do not collect personal identification information, financial information, or location data.

Learning Analytics

Skynet RoadMap may display progress statistics, completion percentages, and productivity insights to help users track their learning journey. All analytics are generated locally using user-entered data.

Internet Usage

Some features of Skynet RoadMap may require internet access for updates or online content. However, basic roadmap tracking works offline.

Children's Privacy

Skynet RoadMap is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
