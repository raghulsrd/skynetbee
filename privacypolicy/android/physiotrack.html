Privacy Policy

This privacy policy governs the use of our application PhysioTrack. The app is designed to help users calculate and monitor their Body Mass Index (BMI) using height and weight details.

Information Collection and Use

Our app does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

The height and weight entered by the user are used only for BMI calculation purposes inside the app.

All calculations are processed locally on your device and no data is sent to any server.

Features of the App

The app may calculate and display:

  • Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Healthy weight range
  • Underweight status
  • Overweight status
  • Obesity level indication
  • Basic health insights based on BMI values

These calculations are generated instantly and are not stored online.

Device Access

PhysioTrack:

  • Does not require account registration
  • Does not access contacts, messages, photos, or sensitive data
  • Does not track your activity
  • May work completely offline depending on app features

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party services that collect personal user data.

If advertisements, analytics, or external services are added in future updates, this privacy policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Security

Since no personal information is stored or transmitted online, your data remains secure on your device.

Children’s Privacy

PhysioTrack is suitable for all age groups. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any future changes will be posted on this page.
