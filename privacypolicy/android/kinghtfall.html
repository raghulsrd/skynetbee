Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Knight Fall handles user information. Knight Fall is built to provide a safe, enjoyable, and fully offline gaming experience while respecting user privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Knight Fall does not collect, store, or share personal user information.

The game may locally store:

  • High scores
  • Game progress
  • Gameplay settings and preferences

All stored data remains only on the user's device.

We do not collect:

  • Personal information
  • Email addresses
  • Location data
  • Financial information
  • Login or account information

2. Data Storage

All game data is securely stored locally on your device. Knight Fall does not upload or transmit user data to external servers.

3. Internet Usage

Knight Fall works completely offline and does not require an internet connection for gameplay.

4. Advertisements

Knight Fall does not display advertisements and does not use advertising services.

5. Third-Party Services

Knight Fall does not use third-party services that collect personal user data.

6. Children's Privacy

Knight Fall is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
