Privacy Policy

Tapzio ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Tapzio handles information while you use the application. Tapzio is an offline multiplayer tapping game designed for entertainment without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information Collection

Tapzio does not collect, store, or share any personally identifiable information. The following gameplay information may be temporarily used while the game is running:

  • Player scores
  • Game progress
  • Current match status
  • Gameplay settings (if available)

This information remains only on your device and is never transmitted to external servers.

Data Processing

All game logic, score calculations, and gameplay processing are performed locally on your device. Tapzio does not upload or synchronize your gameplay data with any online service.

Permissions

Tapzio does not require access to sensitive device permissions such as:

  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • SMS
  • Storage (except when required by Android for normal app operation)

Third-Party Services

Tapzio does not use third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking services that collect personal information. If future versions include advertisements or analytics, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

Tapzio is suitable for players of all ages. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Data Security

Since Tapzio does not collect or store personal information, no user data is stored on our servers.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will appear on this page with an updated revision date.
