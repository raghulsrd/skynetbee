Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Remind Me handles your information. Our application is designed to help users create and manage personal event reminders such as birthdays, anniversaries, meetings, and important dates while respecting your privacy.

1. Information We Store

Remind Me does not collect or transmit your personal information. All reminder details entered into the application remain stored locally on your device.

  • Person names
  • Event names
  • Event dates
  • Images selected for reminders

We do not collect passwords, financial information, contacts, location data, or any sensitive personal information.

2. Data Storage

All reminder information is stored securely on your device using local storage (SQLite database). Your data is not uploaded, shared, or synchronized with external servers.

3. Notifications

The app uses Android notification permission only to remind you about your saved events. Notifications are generated locally on your device and no notification data is sent to any third party.

4. Internet Usage

Remind Me works completely offline. An internet connection is not required for creating, editing, or viewing reminders.

5. Third-Party Services

Remind Me does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or cloud storage that collect your personal information.

6. Children's Privacy

Remind Me is suitable for users of all ages. The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page along with the updated effective date.
