Privacy Policy for Memorix

Last updated: July 8, 2026

Summary: Memorix is a memory-matching game built for players of all ages, including children. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It works fully offline and does not contain ads, in-app purchases, or third-party trackers.

1. Information We Collect

Memorix does not collect any personally identifiable information (PII). We do not require account creation, sign-in, email addresses, or any other personal details to use the app.

The app does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking libraries.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Memorix may store limited gameplay data — such as your best scores, move counts, and completion times — directly on your device using local storage. This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not transmitted to us or any third party
  • Is automatically removed if you uninstall the app

3. Children's Privacy

Memorix is designed to be safe and enjoyable for children. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children or any other users. The app contains no chat features, no social sharing, no external links accessible to the player, and no advertising, in keeping with applicable children's privacy laws and Google Play's Families Policy.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below and we will address the concern promptly — though as noted above, the app is not designed to collect such information in the first place.

4. Permissions

Memorix may request only the minimum device permissions necessary for core functionality (for example, sound playback for game effects). It does not request access to your camera, contacts, location, microphone, or storage beyond what is needed to save your own local game progress.

5. Third-Party Services

Memorix does not integrate any third-party services, advertising networks, or analytics platforms at this time. If this changes in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly, and the update will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

6. Data Security

Since no personal or gameplay data is transmitted off your device, there is no server-side data for us to secure. All data stays local to your device and is protected by your device's own operating system security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app's functionality. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
