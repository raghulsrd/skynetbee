01

Information We Collect

XO Game does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, device identifiers, or usage data — from you or anyone else who plays on your device.

The game has no account system, no sign-in, no analytics, and no network connection of any kind. There's simply nowhere for your information to go, because the app never sends anything anywhere.

02

App Permissions

XO Game does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or any other sensitive device permission. It only needs standard display access to render the game on your screen.

03

Local Data & Storage

The app keeps a small amount of information directly on your device to make gameplay convenient — for example:

  • Your current match score (Fire wins, Ice wins, and Draws)
  • Your selected level / grid size
  • In-progress board state, so undo and new-game work correctly

This information stays entirely on your device. It is never uploaded, backed up to a server, or shared with us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it completely.

04

Third-Party Services

XO Game does not integrate any third-party SDKs — no advertising networks, no analytics platforms, no crash reporting tools, and no social logins. The app you install is the entire app; nothing else runs alongside it.

Note: if a future update ever introduces a third-party service (for instance, optional analytics to fix bugs), this policy will be updated first, and the change will be described clearly here before it takes effect.
05

Children's Privacy

XO Game is suitable for players of all ages, including children, precisely because it collects nothing. Since no personal information is gathered from anyone, there is no personal information collected from children either, consistent with regulations such as COPPA.

06

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app adds a new feature that uses data differently — the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date above. We'd encourage you to check back here occasionally if you'd like to stay informed.

07

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or about XO Game, feel free to reach out.
