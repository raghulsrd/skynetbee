Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application CashVault. This application prioritizes user privacy and is designed to provide a simple and secure business transaction tracking experience for marketers, shop owners, and small business users.

1. Information We Collect

CashVault does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. All sales and purchase records entered in the app remain on the user’s device.

  • Item names
  • Quantity details
  • Purchase amounts
  • Sales amounts
  • Profit and loss calculations

We do not collect personal identification information, contact details, financial information, or location data.

2. Data Storage

All business data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your data to external servers.

3. Analytics and Graphical Reports

CashVault provides histogram-style graphical charts and dashboard summaries to help users assess weekly and monthly profit and loss trends. All analytics are generated locally using user-entered data.

4. Internet Usage

CashVault functions offline and does not require an internet connection to operate.

5. Third-Party Services

CashVault does not use third-party services that collect personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

CashVault is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
