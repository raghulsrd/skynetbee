Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application Roadmap. This application respects user privacy and is designed to help students follow their learning journey, track task marks, and unlock stages in a simple, game-style experience.

1

Information We Collect

Roadmap loads the following information from the SkyNetBee server so it can show your progress:

  • Member ID
  • Name and class
  • Room and system number
  • Task marks
  • Profile photo (only if one is available)

We do not collect contact details, financial information, or location data.

2

Data Storage

Your roadmap progress, such as the task you have reached and the medals you have earned, is stored locally on your device. Clearing the app data or uninstalling the app removes it. Your name, marks, and other records are held on the SkyNetBee server as part of your student records.

3

Sounds and Vibration

Roadmap uses short celebration sounds and device vibration when you complete a stage. No audio is recorded.

4

Internet Usage

Roadmap needs an internet connection to load your profile and marks. The road, stages, and tasks can still be viewed without a connection.

5

Third-Party Services

Roadmap does not use advertising or analytics services, and we do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

6

Children's Privacy

Roadmap is intended for students enrolled with SkyNetBee. We only use the information described above for learning records and do not knowingly collect any other personal information from children.

7

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
