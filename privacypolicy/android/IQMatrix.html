IQ Matrix
Privacy Policy
Welcome to IQ Matrix — a fun memory card matching game designed for kids! We are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains clearly what information we collect (or don't collect), how we use it, and your rights.

Short version: We collect nothing. IQ Matrix is 100% private and safe for children.
📋
1. Information We Collect

IQ Matrix does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal information from its users — including children. We do not require any account creation or login.

  • We do NOT collect your name or email address
  • We do NOT collect your location or GPS data
  • We do NOT collect device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • We do NOT use cookies or tracking technologies
  • We do NOT collect any data from children under 13
  • We do NOT require internet access to play
✅ All game data (scores, progress, settings) is stored only on your device using Android's local database and is never sent anywhere.
👶
2. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliant)

IQ Matrix is designed for children and we take their privacy extremely seriously. We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy.

  • No personal data is collected from children of any age
  • No advertising is shown inside the app
  • No in-app purchases are available
  • No social features, chat or user-generated content
  • No links to external websites within the app
  • Parents do not need to provide consent as no data is collected
🔐
3. App Permissions

IQ Matrix requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function. Here is a full list of all permissions used:

🔊 Audio / Sound — Used to play card flip, match and win sound effects during gameplay. No audio is recorded or transmitted.

No other permissions are requested. IQ Matrix does not access your camera, microphone for recording, contacts, storage, location or any other device feature.
📶
4. Internet Access

IQ Matrix does not require an internet connection to play. The app works fully offline at all times. We do not make any network requests, contact any servers or send any data over the internet.

This means IQ Matrix is completely safe to use on aeroplanes, in areas with no signal, and on devices with parental controls that restrict internet access.
💰
5. Advertising and In-App Purchases
  • IQ Matrix contains NO advertisements of any kind
  • IQ Matrix contains NO in-app purchases
  • IQ Matrix contains NO premium upgrades or subscriptions
  • IQ Matrix does NOT use any third-party advertising SDKs
  • IQ Matrix does NOT use any analytics or crash reporting SDKs

The app is completely free to download and play with no hidden costs.

🔗
6. Third-Party Services

IQ Matrix does not integrate with any third-party services, SDKs, analytics platforms, advertising networks or social media platforms. No data is shared with any third party under any circumstances.

🛡️
7. Data Security

Since IQ Matrix collects no personal data, there is no personal data at risk. Any game data (such as local scores) is stored securely on your device using Android's standard local database (Room Database) and is protected by your device's own security model.

📝
8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
