Privacy Policy

Last updated:

This Privacy Policy explains how the CodeSync mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use it. CodeSync helps developers connect, chat, and share updates with each other — this policy covers exactly what that involves.

In short: CodeSync stores your profile and messages to make chatting possible. Location and contact sharing only happen when you choose to share them inside a chat, and "Delete for Everyone" removes a message from both sides only within 1 hour of sending it.

Information We Collect

To create your profile and let you connect with other developers, CodeSync collects:

  • Profile details — your full name, phone number, role (e.g., Android app developer), and bio
  • Messages — the text, media, and other content you send and receive in chats
  • Connections and posts — developers you connect with, and posts or updates you create

Location Sharing

CodeSync does not track your location in the background. Your location is only accessed and shared with another user when you actively choose to share it inside a chat (for example, sharing your current location with a connection). Once sent, it is delivered as a message like any other and is subject to the same deletion rules below.

Contact Sharing

You may choose to share a contact card from your device with another user inside a chat. CodeSync only accesses your contacts when you open the contact-sharing feature and select a contact to send — it does not read, upload, or store your full contact list otherwise.

Delete for Everyone

"Delete for Everyone" removes a message from both your chat and the recipient's chat, but it is only available within 1 hour of sending that message. After 1 hour, you can still delete the message from your own view, but it will remain visible to the recipient.

How We Use Information

  • To create and display your profile to other developers on the platform
  • To deliver messages, location shares, and contact shares between you and your connections
  • To show your posts and updates to the community
  • To operate features like connections, chat, and notifications

Data Storage and Retention

Messages, shared locations, and shared contacts are stored to keep your chat history available across your devices. If you delete a message for everyone within the 1-hour window, it is removed from both accounts. Uninstalling the App does not automatically delete your account or chat history from our servers — contact us if you'd like your account and data removed.

Permissions

CodeSync may request the following device permissions:

  • Contacts — used only when you choose to share a contact in a chat
  • Location — used only when you choose to share your location in a chat
  • Notifications — used to alert you about new messages, connections, and updates

Age Requirement

CodeSync is intended for users aged 16 and above. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. If we learn that a user under 16 has created an account, we will take steps to remove that account and associated data.

Third-Party Services

CodeSync does not sell your information or share it with advertisers. If we introduce any third-party analytics or services in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information in chats beyond what's necessary.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
