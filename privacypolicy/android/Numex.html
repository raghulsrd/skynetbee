PRIVACY POLICY
Effective Date: June 25, 2025  ·  Last Updated: June 25, 2025  ·  Developer: Kokila — Skynet Bee
📋

01 · OVERVIEW

NUMEX is a Sudoku puzzle game built for Android. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information. We built NUMEX to be a clean, distraction-free experience — and that philosophy extends to data too.

Short version: NUMEX does not collect, store, or share any personal data. Zero.
🚫

02 · DATA WE DO NOT COLLECT

NUMEX does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data (GPS, IP-based location, or otherwise)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Camera, microphone, or contacts access
  • Social media login or account information
📱

03 · LOCAL GAME DATA

NUMEX may store minimal data locally on your device to support gameplay — such as your selected difficulty level or current board state. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your device
  • Is never transmitted to any server
  • Is removed when you uninstall the app
🌐

04 · INTERNET & NETWORK

NUMEX does not require an internet connection to function. The app does not make any network requests, connect to remote servers, or use third-party SDKs that transmit data online.

🔗

05 · THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

NUMEX does not integrate any third-party SDKs, advertising networks, analytics platforms, or social media plugins. There are no in-app purchases, ads, or external services of any kind.

👶

06 · CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

NUMEX is suitable for all ages. Since we collect no personal information whatsoever, there is no risk to users of any age — including children under 13. The app fully complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

🔐

07 · ANDROID PERMISSIONS

NUMEX requests no sensitive Android permissions. The app does not ask for access to:

  • Camera or microphone
  • Contacts or call logs
  • Storage / files on your device
  • Location services
  • Network state or internet access
📝

08 · POLICY CHANGES

If this Privacy Policy is updated in the future, the new version will be posted here with a revised "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We'll keep it just as straightforward.
