Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application Tiny Explorers. Tiny Explorers is a preschool educational learning application designed for children aged 3–6 years. Our application highly prioritizes user privacy and is designed to provide a fun, safe, child-friendly, and enjoyable environment that encourages early learning.

1. Information We Collect

Tiny Explorers does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. The app is completely safe for children and does not gather any identifiable details during interactions with learning modules such as:

  • Alphabets & Numbers
  • Animals & Birds
  • Fruits & Vegetables
  • Colors & Shapes
  • Flowers & Vehicles

We do not collect personal identification information, contact details, device unique identifiers, or location data. All interactive data (such as card flips or selected audio options) remains temporary.

2. Data Storage

Tiny Explorers operates perfectly without storing personal data. Any learning progress or local preference settings are stored strictly on your local device. We do not upload, host, or transmit any user information to external servers.

3. Audio and Interactive Features

Tiny Explorers uses clear audio pronunciation and animations to facilitate object recognition and vocabulary building. All graphic animations, colorful illustrations, and sound effects are processed locally and do not require background user data recording.

4. Internet Usage

Tiny Explorers functions completely offline and does not require an active internet connection to operate. This ensures that your child is safe from unexpected online interactions or external data exchanges.

5. Third-Party Services

Tiny Explorers does not integrate third-party trackers, external analytical toolkits, or target-based third-party advertisements that collect personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

As an educational app targeted at children aged 3–6 years, Tiny Explorers is compliant with strict child privacy principles. We do not knowingly prompt, ask for, or collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be updated and reflected on this page.
