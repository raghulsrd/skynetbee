Privacy Policy

CGPA Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") respects your privacy. This application is designed to calculate your CGPA quickly and accurately without collecting or storing your personal information.

Information Collection

The app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. The following data entered by users remains only on the device:

  • Total number of subjects
  • Subject credits
  • Subject grades
  • Calculated CGPA results

Data Processing

All CGPA calculations are performed locally on your device. No academic information is transmitted to any server or external service.

Permissions

CGPA Calculator does not require access to sensitive permissions such as contacts, location, camera, microphone, or storage for its core functionality.

Third-Party Services

This application does not use third-party services to collect personal data. If future versions include analytics or advertisements, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
