NEON BOX

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Neon Box mobile application ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it. We built Neon Box to be simple and self-contained — please read below for the specifics.

In short: Neon Box does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information. All game data (your settings and statistics) stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Neon Box does not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App does not:

  • Require account creation, sign-in, or any personal identifiers
  • Collect your name, email address, or location
  • Use analytics or tracking SDKs
  • Display third-party advertisements
  • Transmit any data to remote servers

Data Stored on Your Device

The App uses your device's local storage (Android SharedPreferences) to save the following information only on your device:

  • Game settings (e.g., sound and vibration preferences, default board size and difficulty)
  • Game statistics (e.g., games played, won, lost, drawn, win streaks, high scores)
  • Daily challenge progress and results

This data never leaves your device, is not linked to your identity, and is not accessible to us or any third party. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes this data.

Permissions

Neon Box may request the following device permission:

  • Vibration — used only to provide haptic feedback when you complete a box during gameplay. It is not used for any other purpose.

Children's Privacy

Neon Box does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since the App collects no personal data at all, it may be used by players of any age in compliance with applicable children's privacy laws (such as COPPA).

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-collection services. If this changes in a future update, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device rather than on external servers, your information is only as secure as your device itself. We recommend keeping your device's operating system and security settings up to date.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
