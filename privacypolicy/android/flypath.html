Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 19, 2026

FlyPath ("we", "our", "us", or "the app") respects your privacy. FlyPath is an offline flight-path and globe application designed to provide its core features directly on your Android device.

FlyPath is an offline app. The core features of FlyPath do not require an internet connection. Your flight-path calculations, airport information, 3D globe, and GPS-based current-location features are designed to work directly on your device.

1. Offline Operation

FlyPath is designed to work without an internet connection. The application includes the information required for its core flight-path functionality within the app itself.

You can enter departure and arrival airport codes and view the corresponding flight path on the 3D globe without requiring mobile data or Wi-Fi.

2. Location Information

FlyPath may request precise location permission from your Android device. This permission is used to obtain your current GPS location and display your position on the globe.

FlyPath uses the Android GPS provider for location information. GPS positioning itself does not require an internet connection.

3. How Location Is Used

Your device location may be used for the following features:

  • Displaying your current location on the 3D globe.
  • Automatically centering the globe on your location.
  • Updating your location marker as your GPS position changes.
  • Updating the airplane position based on your location.
  • Using available GPS speed and bearing information to determine direction.

4. Location Data Is Processed Locally

Location information used by FlyPath is processed locally on your Android device for the application's features.

FlyPath does not intentionally upload your GPS coordinates to a FlyPath server or maintain a server-side database of your location history.

5. Personal Information

FlyPath does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, address, or password to use the core application.

6. Airport Information

FlyPath uses locally available airport information to identify departure and arrival airports and calculate the flight path.

Airport codes entered into the application are processed locally for the flight-path functionality.

7. Internet Connection

An internet connection is not required for the core features of FlyPath.

However, FlyPath contains optional links to external services such as LinkedIn and Instagram in the developer information section. If you tap one of these links, your device may open the corresponding website or application, which may require an internet connection.

8. Data Sharing

FlyPath does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your personal information or GPS location with third parties.

9. Permissions

FlyPath may request the following Android permission:

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — used to obtain precise GPS location for the current-location and flight-path features.

FlyPath does not require contacts, camera, microphone, or SMS permissions for its core functionality.

10. Children's Privacy

FlyPath does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The application does not require users to create an account or submit personal information.

11. Data Security

Because FlyPath is designed to process its core functionality locally and does not intentionally upload GPS information to FlyPath servers, the application does not maintain a server-side database of your GPS location history.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application's features, permissions, or data practices change. Any changes will be reflected on this page together with a new "Last Updated" date.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or FlyPath, please contact the developer.
