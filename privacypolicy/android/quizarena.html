QuizArena Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

QuizArena ("we", "our", "the app") is a general knowledge quiz app for Android, built by Ashmitha, an IT student, for students and quiz lovers. This Privacy Policy explains how QuizArena handles your information. Your privacy matters to us.

1. Information We Collect

QuizArena does not collect, store, or share any personal user data. The only information the app keeps is your quiz activity, and it stays on your device:

  • Quiz scores
  • Selected categories
  • Answer streaks and progress
  • App settings

We do not collect personal identification information, contact details, financial information, or location data.

2. Data Storage

All quiz progress and settings are stored locally on your device. We do not upload or transmit your data to external servers. Uninstalling the app removes this data.

3. Quiz Content

QuizArena offers quiz categories such as Chemistry, Physics, Flags, National Symbols, Sports, Math Formulas, Tech, Grammar, Tamil and Fun Questions. All questions are built into the app and are used only to provide the quiz experience.

4. Internet Usage

QuizArena functions offline and does not require an internet connection to operate.

5. Third-Party Services

QuizArena does not use third-party services that collect personal data.

6. Children's Privacy

QuizArena is suitable for students, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
