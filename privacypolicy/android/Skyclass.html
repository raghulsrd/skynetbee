🎓 Sky Class
Privacy Policy

Your classroom data, handled with the same care as a roll-call register.

Sky Class is a digital classroom app for teachers and students — handling chats, attendance, marks, and study materials. This page explains exactly what we collect, why, and how you stay in control of it.

🏠

01. Overview

Sky Class lets teachers create classrooms and lets students join them using a class code or invite link. Inside a classroom, people can chat, mark and view attendance, manage tests and marks, and share study materials such as notes, PDFs, and assignments.

This Privacy Policy applies to everyone who uses Sky Class — whether you've signed up as a teacher who creates classrooms, or a student who joins one. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

🗂️

02. Information we collect

We only collect what's needed to run your classrooms — nothing else.

Profile

Account & profile details

Name, phone number, college/school name, and an optional profile photo, as shown on your "My Profile" screen.

Classroom

Classroom data

Classroom names, class PINs/codes, subject names, and the list of members in each classroom you create or join.

Activity

Chats & messages

Messages, announcements, and discussion content posted inside a classroom's chat section.

Academic

Attendance & marks

Attendance records (present/absent) and test names, subjects, and marks entered for each student.

Files

Study materials

Notes, PDFs, images, and assignments uploaded by teachers into a classroom's materials section.

Technical

Device & usage data

Basic technical information like app version, device type, and crash logs, used to keep the app running smoothly.

⚙️

03. How we use your information

We use the information above strictly to run the features you see in the app:

Profile info→ Identify you in classrooms→ Chat, attendance & marks→ Progress tracking
  • Operate classrooms — creating and joining classes, displaying member lists, and powering chat, attendance, and marks features.
  • Show your profile — displaying your name, phone number, and college on your profile and to classmates/teachers where relevant.
  • Track academic progress — recording attendance and test marks so teachers and students can monitor performance over time.
  • Share materials — letting teachers upload and students access notes, assignments, and PDFs inside a classroom.
  • Maintain & improve the app — diagnosing crashes, fixing bugs, and understanding which features are used.
  • Communicate with you — sending in-app notifications (like the bell icon) about classroom activity, such as new messages or announcements.

We do not use your classroom data, chats, marks, or attendance records to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell this information to third parties.

🧑‍🏫

04. Teacher vs. student data

Sky Class has two everyday roles, and what each role can see is different. Here's a quick breakdown:

DataTeacher (Admin)Student
Create / delete classroom Yes No
View full student list Yes Limited
Mark attendance Yes View only
Enter / edit marks Yes View own marks
Upload study materials Yes Download only
Post in classroom chat Yes Yes

A teacher who creates a classroom is shown as its Admin and can manage attendance, tests, marks, and materials for that classroom. Students who join only see the data relevant to classrooms they're a member of.

🔗

05. Sharing & classroom visibility

Sky Class is built around classrooms, so some information is, by design, visible to other people in the same classroom:

  • Within a classroom: your name, messages, attendance status, and marks may be visible to the teacher (admin) and, in some views, to fellow students — for example, a marks list showing every student's name.
  • Outside your classrooms: people who are not members of a classroom cannot see its chats, attendance, marks, or materials.
  • Service providers: we may use trusted infrastructure providers (such as cloud hosting and storage services) to operate the app. They process data only on our behalf and under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Sky Class, its users, or others.

We do not share your personal data with advertisers, and we do not sell classroom data, chat content, attendance, or marks to any third party.

🔒

06. Storage & security

Classroom data — including chats, attendance, marks, and materials — is stored on secure servers used to power the app. We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures (such as access controls and encrypted connections) to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or loss.

That said, no method of storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to keep your account credentials private and to log out of shared devices.

🎒

07. Children's privacy

Sky Class is used in school and college settings, and some students may be minors. Where a student is a minor, we collect only the information needed for classroom features (such as name and attendance/marks records), and rely on the educational institution or a parent/guardian to oversee the student's use of the app in line with local laws.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with information beyond what's needed for classroom use, please contact us using the details below so we can review and, where appropriate, remove it.

🛡️

08. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have some or all of the following rights over your personal information:

👁️Access your data
✏️Correct inaccurate info
🗑️Request deletion
📦Export your data

You can update your name, phone number, and college directly from the Edit Profile screen at any time. For anything else — like deleting your account or removing classroom records — reach out to us via the contact section below.

⏳

09. Data retention & deletion

We retain classroom data — including attendance, marks, chats, and materials — for as long as the classroom exists or as needed to provide the app's features, such as tracking academic progress across a term.

  • When a teacher deletes a classroom, its associated attendance, marks, chat, and materials data is removed from active use.
  • When you delete your account, we remove or anonymize your personal profile information, except where we're required to retain it for legal or legitimate administrative purposes.
  • Backups may persist for a limited period after deletion before being permanently purged from our systems.
🔄

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, our practices, or legal requirements. When we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page, and where appropriate, notify you in-app.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.
