Privacy Policy · Last Updated: August 2026

Summary: Agaram is a fully offline Tamil-learning app. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. There are no user accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

🔒 No Data Collected 📴 Works Fully Offline 🚫 No Ads 🚫 No Tracking

📖 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Agaram ("the App," "we," "our") handles user information. Agaram is developed and maintained by Samishragav K, and was built to help learners explore Tamil through interactive quizzes, letter flashcards, and missing-word puzzles — with privacy in mind from the start.

🗂️ Information We Collect

Agaram does not collect any personal information. Specifically, the App does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or contact details
  • Account or login credentials — no account is required to use the App
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers used for tracking or advertising
  • Photos, contacts, or other device data

Any progress you make in the App (such as completed levels, scores, or stars earned) is stored only locally on your own device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

📢 Advertising and Analytics

Agaram does not display advertisements and does not use any third-party analytics, tracking, or crash-reporting services. No data about your usage of the App ever leaves your device.

🔗 Third-Party Services

Agaram does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, APIs, or services that collect data. The App functions entirely offline, from the letter flashcards to the missing-word puzzles.

🧒 Children's Privacy

Because Agaram does not collect any personal information from anyone, it is safe for learners of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect data from children, as we do not collect data from anyone.

🛡️ Data Security

Since no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk of being accessed, lost, or misused through use of this App.

✅ Your Rights

As we do not collect or store any personal data, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, or delete on your behalf. Any local progress data can be removed at any time by uninstalling the App or clearing its data through your device settings.

🔄 Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if new features are added to the App that change how data is handled. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
