Privacy Policy

Astro Bird

Welcome to Astro Bird!

Astro Bird is a voice-controlled arcade game where players use their voice to control a flying bird. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Microphone Permission

Astro Bird requires access to your device's microphone.

The microphone is used only to detect your voice or sound while playing the game. This allows the bird to fly based on the volume of your voice.

We do not:

  • Record your voice.
  • Store audio recordings.
  • Upload your voice to any server.
  • Share your voice with anyone.

All voice processing happens locally on your device.

2. Personal Information

Astro Bird does not collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Location
  • Contacts
  • Photos
  • Messages

The Developer Info page inside the app only displays the developer's contact details for user reference. It is not used to collect any user information.

3. Game Data

The app may store game-related information on your device, such as:

  • Best Score
  • Last Score

This information is stored locally on your device and is not shared online.

4. Internet Connection

Astro Bird can be played without an internet connection.

The app does not require an online account to play.

5. Third-Party Services

Currently, Astro Bird does not use third-party services to collect personal information.

If future versions include services such as Google Play Games, Google AdMob, Firebase, or other third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

6. Children's Privacy

Astro Bird is suitable for players of all ages.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Since Astro Bird does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information transmitted to our servers.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be reflected in this Privacy Policy whenever necessary.
