Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application Pixsnap. Pixsnap allows users to capture photos using their device camera or select images from their gallery and instantly convert them into pixel-art images. Users can save and share the generated images.

Information Collection and Use

Pixsnap does not collect, store, or transmit personal information to our servers.

Users may capture photos using their device camera or select existing images from their device gallery for pixel-art conversion.

All image processing is performed locally on the user's device. Images are not uploaded to external servers.

App Features

The application provides the following features:

  • Capture photos using the device camera
  • Select images from the device gallery
  • Convert images into pixel-art format
  • Save generated pixel-art images to the device
  • Share generated images using device sharing options

Device Permissions

Pixsnap may request permission to:

  • Access the device camera for taking photos
  • Access the device gallery or storage for selecting images
  • Save generated images to the device
  • Share generated images through supported applications

These permissions are used solely for the functionality described above.

Image Processing

Images selected or captured by users are processed locally on the device. We do not upload, analyze, store, or share these images on any remote server.

Third-Party Services

Pixsnap does not use third-party services to process user images.

If analytics, advertisements, cloud storage, or other third-party services are added in future updates, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

Data Security

Since image processing occurs locally on your device and images are not transmitted to our servers, your data remains under your control.

Children's Privacy

Pixsnap is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
