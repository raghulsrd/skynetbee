Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to TrackIt. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles user information and data.

1. Information We Collect

To use TrackIt, users are required to provide:

  • User name

Users may optionally provide:

  • Profile photo

The app also stores user-created habit tracking information, including:

  • Habit names
  • Habit completion records
  • Daily progress
  • Graphs and statistics
  • App preferences

2. How Your Information Is Used

The collected information is used only for providing app features and improving your experience.

  • Tracking habits
  • Displaying graphs and analytics
  • Saving user progress
  • Personalizing the app experience

3. Local Database Storage

All data in TrackIt is stored locally on your device using a local database.

We do not upload your personal data or habit records to external servers.

4. Profile Photos

Adding a profile photo is completely optional. If provided, the image remains stored only on your device.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer your personal information or app data to third parties.

6. Permissions

The app may request limited permissions only when necessary, such as selecting a profile image from your gallery or camera.

TrackIt does not access:

  • Contacts
  • Messages
  • Call logs
  • Location data

7. Data Security

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you remain in control of your information.

We take reasonable steps to protect user data from unauthorized access.

8. Children's Privacy

TrackIt is suitable for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy in the future. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
