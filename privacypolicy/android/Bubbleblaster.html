Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy governs the use of our application Bubble Game. This game is designed to provide users with a fun and entertaining bubble popping experience with exciting levels, scores, and challenges.

1. Information We Collect

Bubble Game does not collect, store, or share sensitive personal information from users. Game progress and scores are stored locally on the user's device.

  • Game scores
  • Level progress
  • Star ratings
  • Game settings
  • Achievement progress

We do not collect personal identification, financial details, or precise location data.

2. Data Storage

All game-related data is stored locally on your device. We do not upload or share your personal gameplay data to external servers.

3. Gameplay Analytics

Bubble Game may display statistics such as high scores, completed levels, and star ratings to improve the gaming experience. All analytics are generated locally.
