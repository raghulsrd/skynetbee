CipherNest

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

CipherNest is a privacy-focused password manager designed to help users securely store and manage their passwords and other sensitive information on their Android device.

Your privacy is a core part of CipherNest's design. This Privacy Policy explains what information CipherNest accesses, how that information is stored, and what information is not collected.

Privacy by design: CipherNest is designed to keep your password data on your device. Your stored passwords are not uploaded to a CipherNest server.

2. Information We Collect

CipherNest does not require you to create an online account and does not collect your stored passwords, notes, credentials, or other vault contents on our servers.

CipherNest is designed not to collect:

  • Passwords stored in your vault
  • Website usernames or login credentials
  • Secure notes stored in the vault
  • Your master password or authentication secret
  • Contacts, photos, messages, or unrelated personal files
  • Your precise location

3. Local Storage and Encryption

CipherNest stores your password-manager data locally on your Android device. Sensitive information is protected using security mechanisms provided by Android and encryption technologies implemented by the application.

CipherNest may use the Android Keystore and encrypted local storage mechanisms to protect cryptographic keys and sensitive application data.

Because your vault is stored locally, protecting your device, screen lock, master password, and biometric credentials is important to the security of your data.

4. Master Password and Biometric Authentication

Your master password is used to protect access to your vault. CipherNest does not send your master password to a remote server.

If you enable biometric authentication, CipherNest relies on Android's biometric authentication system. CipherNest does not receive or store your actual fingerprint, face, or other biometric data.

5. Password Generation

CipherNest may provide tools for generating strong passwords. Generated passwords are created within the application and are not transmitted to CipherNest servers as part of the password generation feature.

6. Internet Access

CipherNest is designed around local storage and does not require an online account or cloud server to store your password vault.

If a future version introduces an online feature, this Privacy Policy will be updated to clearly describe the information involved before such a feature is used.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Tracking

CipherNest does not intentionally sell, rent, or share your password-vault information with advertisers or data brokers.

CipherNest is not designed to use your stored passwords or vault contents for advertising, profiling, or targeted marketing.

8. Third-Party Services

CipherNest is designed to minimize dependency on third-party services that could access sensitive vault information.

Android system services, including the Android Keystore and biometric authentication framework, may be used to provide security functionality. These system components operate according to their respective platform policies.

9. Data Sharing

We do not sell or trade your stored password-manager data. Since CipherNest is designed to keep vault information locally on your device, CipherNest does not have access to the contents of your local vault through a remote storage system.

10. Data Deletion

Because your vault data is stored locally, you can remove it by deleting the corresponding data from your device or uninstalling CipherNest, subject to Android's storage behavior and any backups created by you or your device.

If you manually export, back up, copy, or otherwise transfer your data outside CipherNest, those copies may remain available until you delete them yourself.

11. Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect CipherNest and its local data. However, no software or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your Android device updated, use a strong device lock, protect your master password, and avoid sharing access to your device with untrusted people.

12. Children's Privacy

CipherNest is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Since CipherNest does not require an online account to store a vault, it does not intentionally collect children's personal information through a remote service.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CipherNest's features, security practices, or legal requirements change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or CipherNest's privacy practices, please contact us through the official contact information provided by the CipherNest developer.

15. Disclaimer

CipherNest is provided as a password-management tool and is not a substitute for maintaining the security of your Android device, account credentials, or backups. Users are responsible for maintaining access to their device and master password.
