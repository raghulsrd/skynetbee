2. Information Used by Sky Chrono

Sky Chrono uses gameplay-related information necessary to provide the game's functionality.

  • Current level
  • Completed levels
  • Unlocked levels
  • Last completed level
  • Last exited level
  • Current level progress
  • Shards collected
  • Total shards collected
  • Level result information
  • Game progress
  • Sound preferences
  • Master Sound preference
  • Background Music preference
  • Game Sounds preference

This information is used to provide gameplay functionality such as continuing a level, unlocking levels, displaying progress, showing results, and managing game settings.

3. Gameplay Features

Sky Chrono includes:

  • 150 playable levels
  • Spaceship movement
  • Joystick controls
  • Increasing ship speed as gameplay progresses
  • Shards to collect
  • Obstacles
  • Portals
  • Timed gameplay
  • Level progression
  • Level completion requirements
  • Level retry functionality
  • Next-level progression
  • Resume-level functionality
  • Home and menu navigation
  • Gameplay results
  • Progress tracking
  • A final completion screen after all 150 levels are completed

4. Game Progress

Sky Chrono tracks gameplay progress so that players can continue their game.

  • Current level
  • Last exited level
  • Last completed level
  • Completed level count
  • Unlocked level
  • Shard count
  • Total shards collected
  • Level result information

The App may use this information to allow a player to resume a level or continue to the next unlocked level.

5. Progress Reset

Sky Chrono provides a Progress Reset feature.

When a player chooses to reset game progress, gameplay-related progress may be reset, including completed levels, unlocked levels, current level, last completed level, last exited level, collected shard progress, total shard progress, and level result information.

Resetting game progress does not mean that other information held by third-party services, if any, is automatically deleted.

6. Sound Settings

Sky Chrono provides controls for Master Sound, Background Music, and Game Sounds. These settings are used to control the audio experience within the game.
