Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 17, 2026

Welcome to Sky Chrono. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is received, collected, and how it is used.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use our application, we may collect the following types of data:

  • Voluntary Data: Information you explicitly provide to us, such as account registration names, email addresses, or profile settings.
  • Automated & Device Data: Information automatically provided by your device, including your mobile device ID, operating system version, system language, and general time-zone data.
  • Time & Log Data: As a time-keeping or scheduling application, we track session durations, time stamps, and application performance metrics to maintain service functionality.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the data we collect to maintain, secure, and enhance your user experience. Specifically, your data helps us to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain application functionality.
  • Troubleshoot technical crashes and optimize app load speeds.
  • Ensure system security and prevent unauthorized access or fraudulent activity.

3. Data Retention and Security

We implement appropriate physical, technical, and administrative safeguards designed to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, modification, or disclosure. Your information is only retained for as long as necessary to fulfill the service requirements or comply with legal responsibilities.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal identification information to third parties. We may share anonymized or aggregated analytics data with trusted service providers to help us analyze how our service is used.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may hold rights regarding your data under frameworks like GDPR or CCPA. These include the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you.
