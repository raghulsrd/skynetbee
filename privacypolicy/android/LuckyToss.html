Lucky Toss Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Lucky Toss handles user information and protects user privacy.


Lucky Toss is designed for entertainment purposes only and respects all user privacy rights.

1. Information Collection

Lucky Toss does not collect personal user information.


  • Team names entered in the app
  • Coin toss results
  • Basic local app preferences

All information stays only on your device and is never shared online.

2. Internet Usage

Lucky Toss works completely offline.


The app does not require internet access for normal functionality.

3. Third-Party Services

Lucky Toss does not use third-party services that collect personal user data.

4. Children's Privacy

Lucky Toss is safe for all age groups and does not knowingly collect information from children.

5. Data Security

Since Lucky Toss stores information locally on the device, users maintain full control over their data.
