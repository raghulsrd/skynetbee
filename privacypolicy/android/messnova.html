MessNova is a smart hostel food management and student engagement application that connects Students, Wardens and Administrators. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and protected when you use MessNova.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features enabled in the app, MessNova may process the following information:

Personal and account information

  • Student or staff name and roll number or institutional identifier
  • User role (Student, Warden or Admin)
  • Login and authentication information
  • Profile information provided by the user or institution

Food polling information

  • Selected food preferences and poll responses
  • Poll participation records and voting date/time
  • Food session and poll details

Complaint information

  • Complaint category and description
  • Submission date/time, status and responses

Technical information

Depending on the services and diagnostics enabled, the app or its service providers may process device, app-version, crash, diagnostic and notification-delivery information.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to authenticate users, provide role-specific dashboards, operate food polls, record participation, prevent duplicate votes, manage complaints, send hostel announcements, prepare authorized reports, maintain security and improve app performance.

We do not sell users' personal information.

3. Food Polling and Participation

MessNova records poll responses to calculate results and participation. Records may include the user's institutional identifier, selected food option, poll session and voting timestamp. Authorized Wardens and Administrators may access relevant participation reports to manage hostel dining.

4. Complaints

When a user submits a complaint, the information provided is used to review, assign, respond to and track the complaint. Authorized Wardens and Administrators may access complaint details as required for resolution.

5. User Roles and Data Access

Students: May access their own account, available polls, voting features and complaint tracking.

Wardens: May access authorized student information, participation reports and complaints within their assigned permissions.

Administrators: May manage accounts, polls, complaints, notifications, settings and authorized reports.

Access should be restricted through backend authentication and role-based permissions, not merely by hiding screens or buttons.

6. Notifications

MessNova may send notifications about new polls, poll reminders and results, complaint confirmations and updates, Warden responses and important hostel announcements. Device notification permissions can be managed through the device settings.

7. Data Storage and Security

MessNova uses its configured authentication, database and hosting services to operate the application. Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Third-Party Services

MessNova may rely on third-party providers for authentication, database hosting, notifications, analytics or app operations, where those services are enabled. Such providers may process information needed to provide their services and are subject to their own privacy terms.

The app owner should identify the actual providers used by the production version of MessNova and update this policy where necessary.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, institutional requirements and applicable law. Users may contact the app administrator to request account deactivation or deletion. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate institutional recordkeeping.

10. Children's Privacy

MessNova is intended for authorized hostel residents and institutional users. Where users are minors, the institution and relevant guardians should ensure that appropriate authorization and applicable privacy requirements are followed. The app should collect only information necessary for its intended services.

11. User Responsibilities

  • Keep login credentials confidential.
  • Provide accurate information.
  • Do not share accounts or attempt unauthorized access.
  • Report suspected account misuse to the institution or app administrator.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, service providers, security practices or legal requirements change. The revised policy and update date will be published on this page.
