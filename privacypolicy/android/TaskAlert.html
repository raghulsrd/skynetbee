Privacy Policy
🗄️
Information Collection and Storage

Task Alert is engineered as a privacy-first, local-only application:

No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, scrape, track, or share any personally identifiable information (PII) such as names, email addresses, phone numbers, or hardware IDs.
Local-Only Storage: Any reminder titles, notes, timestamps, or system configuration preferences you input are saved exclusively inside your device's isolated hardware sandbox using Android DataStore mechanisms. This data never exits your device.
🔐
Android System Permissions

To deliver core background functionality reliably, Task Alert requests the following specific operating system flags:

POST_NOTIFICATIONS
Allows the application to display heads-up task alerts and lock-screen banners when reminders are due.
RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
Allows the application's background receiver components to restore and re-schedule your active task timers automatically whenever your device reboots.

Third-Party Services
This application does not integrate with any tracking SDKs, advertising monetization platforms, or remote diagnostic analytic frameworks. Your planning data remains completely isolated.
