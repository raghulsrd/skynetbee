Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how VoxNotes handles user information.

VoxNotes is a simple and easy-to-use notes application that allows users to create voice notes and text notes.

Information Collection and Use

VoxNotes does not collect, store, or share personal information.

All notes created inside the app are stored locally on your device.

We do not upload your notes, recordings, or personal content to external servers.

Features of VoxNotes

The app provides the following features:

  • Create and save voice notes
  • Play voice notes similar to WhatsApp voice messages
  • Create text notes for thoughts and reminders
  • Edit and delete text notes anytime
  • Immediate delete option for voice notes
  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Fast and easy note management

Microphone Permission

VoxNotes requests microphone permission only for recording voice notes.

The microphone is accessed only when the user chooses to record audio inside the app.

Third-Party APIs

VoxNotes may use audio recording APIs provided by the operating system or trusted services to enable voice recording functionality.

These APIs are used only for recording and playback purposes and do not collect personal user information.

Device Access

VoxNotes does not:

  • Require account registration
  • Access contacts, photos, videos, or messages
  • Track user activity
  • Collect sensitive personal data
  • Access location information

Data Security

Since all notes are stored locally on your device, your data remains under your control.

We take reasonable measures to keep the app secure and reliable.

Children’s Privacy

VoxNotes is suitable for users of all ages.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children or any users.

This Privacy Policy may be updated in future versions of the app.

Any updates will be posted on this page.
