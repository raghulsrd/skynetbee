Privacy Policy

The application collects only basic information such as names and project-related details. This information is provided by developers and is used solely for assigning and managing projects.

All collected data is used strictly for internal project management purposes. The information is not shared with external parties and is not used for marketing or advertising.

Access and Control

The data collected is accessible only to authorized developers for project assignment and management purposes.

This application is intended for developer use and is not designed for children under the age of 13.
