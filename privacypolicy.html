Privacy Policy

This privacy policy governs the use of our application Sky Attendance. This application prioritizes user privacy and is designed to provide a simple and secure attendance tracking experience for students.

Information Collection and Use

Our app collects limited user information to provide its core functionality.

  • Name and email address (via Google Sign-In)
  • Basic academic details (such as student information)
  • Attendance data (daily, monthly, and yearly records)

This information is used only for:

  • Displaying attendance details
  • Showing analytics such as bar graphs and circular progress
  • Managing user profiles within the app

We do not sell, trade, or share any personal data with third parties.

Device Access

  • Internet access (required only during login and data synchronization)

Once the user completes the login and initial data synchronization, the app can be used to view attendance data without requiring an internet connection.

The app does not access contacts, messages, or unrelated personal data on the device.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user information. Data may be stored securely using trusted services (such as cloud databases).

However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children’s Privacy

This application is intended for students and educational use. We do not knowingly collect unnecessary personal data from children.

The app is designed to provide a safe and secure environment without misuse of user information.
